The parallel program's 2026-08-31 group fact sharpens the second issue: the continuous stabilizer of `diag(-g, 1, delta, 0)` in SO(1,3) is trivial (the discrete part is the Klein four-group, the OpenWave R9 receipt), so NO exact S^1 fixes the certified vacuum. Any clock on this vacuum is either rigid (extensive inertia) or weighted by hand (kinematic only, `w -> c w` rescales `omega*` freely). A localized clock therefore needs one of: a degenerate eigenvalue pair somewhere (a vacuum with `d2 = d3`, or a wall where it holds), an added field whose orbit norm vanishes in the vacuum, or a different action.
